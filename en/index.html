First steps

Add your first animal. In under a minute.

No account, no sign-up. Tap plus, enter name and sex, done. Cornnector does the rest from a few details.

Type the genetics, get the morph

Type “Shatter” and Cornnector sets Cinder and Sunkissed. Tapping a gene switches between visual, het and possible het. The app builds the morph name itself, and the calculator knows the animal right away.

Link the parents, grow the lineage

Bought animals get their breeder and the parents’ genetics. Your own hatchlings are created straight from the clutch when they hatch, with parents and siblings in the lineage.

Pick the prey, the schedule runs

Set prey and interval, and the hub shows every day who is due. Feeding is a swipe, and a shed pauses the schedule.

Collection

Go through your collection with your thumb.

The animal cards are built so you don’t have to open anything. Right from the list you can:

  • swipe left to feedThe prey set in the profile is preselected, otherwise the one from last time.
  • swipe right to mark “in shed”Spotted blue eyes? The animal is in shed and won’t show up as due for feeding until then. Swipe right again once the skin is off.
  • long press opens the animal menuAll actions explained under The animal menu. A short tap opens the profile.
  • switch between list and tiles
  • search by ID, morph, gene or breeder and filter by sex

The feeding round

Whether two animals or forty.

Every enclosure gets a label with a QR code. Scan, swipe and move on to the next enclosure. No searching, no typing.

Start a round

Tap the QR button at the top and switch on “Feeding round”.

The animal appears full screen with its status and last feeding. Your regular phone camera also opens Cornnector right at the animal.

Swipe and move on

After feeding, the scanner jumps back by itself. The counter shows how many animals are done.

Pairing and clutches

Every pairing. Not just the last one.

Several pairings per season are normal. Cornnector saves each one and takes care of the rest when the clutch arrives.

  • “Paired with …” in the profile, including external animals
  • see the expected offspring before you even save
  • entry in the history of both animals
  • a new clutch takes over the sire and pairing dateThe hatch forecast uses the incubation time of the same dam’s earlier clutches.
  • count slugs, see the dam’s breeding recordSlugs are infertile eggs. They don’t count for incubation but do count in the female’s record: clutches, Ø eggs, Ø slugs, slug rate, hatch rate and Ø incubation time.

More in Cornnector

Labels

A QR code for every enclosure

Label with QR code, name, morph and sex, one at a time or for a selection. The label belongs to the animal and moves with it.

Lineage

Where it comes from, where it goes

Parents, siblings from the same clutch and your own offspring. Bought animals show their parents with the genetics you know from the breeder.

Import

Bring your collection over from Excel

Download the template, enter your animals, import. A preview shows every row and flags gene typos before anything is saved.

Backup

Your data, your backup

No server, no account. So: export a backup and keep it in Dropbox or Google Drive. On a new phone, simply import it again.

Feeding

The right prey for each animal

In the profile you set what the animal gets, from single pinky to adult mouse. Swipe feeding uses it automatically.

Settings

Everything occasional behind the gear

Top right: appearance, language, backup, CSV import and defaults for incubation and feeding interval. You can also bring back the gesture tips there.

Animal profile

Tap fields directly

Change name, morph, sex, origin or feeding interval in the profile with a single tap. Everything can be undone after saving.

History

Swipe mistakes away

Every feeding, shed, measurement and pairing is in the history with its date. Wrong entry? Swipe it left, gone.

FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and get going. Your data lives on your phone, not on someone else’s servers.

Does Cornnector work offline?

Yes. Everything is on your phone. You can feed and log even in a basement without reception.

Can I move to a new phone?

Yes. Export a backup, import it on the new phone, done. All animals, histories and clutches are back.

Can I bring my collection over from Excel?

Yes. There’s a template in the settings. Enter your animals and import the file. A preview checks every row first.

Can I feed several animals at once?

Yes. In the hub you mark all due animals as fed with one tap. Or you go from enclosure to enclosure with the feeding round.

How do I delete a wrong entry?

In the animal profile under “History”, swipe the entry left. Right after that you can take it back with “Undo”.

Why is the ring blinking?

The animal is past its feeding interval. You set the interval in the profile under “Feeding interval”.
