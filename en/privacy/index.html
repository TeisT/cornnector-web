2. In short

The Cornnector app does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter in the app stays on your device. This website uses no cookies, no tracking and no advertising.

3. The Cornnector app

Everything you enter in the app, i.e. animals, feedings, sheds, weights, pairings, clutches, photos and your first name for the greeting, is stored only locally in the app on your device. There is no account and no sign-in. The app does not transmit any data to us or to third parties and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising services.

  • Camera: used only when you scan a QR code or take a photo. The pictures stay on your device.
  • Photos: the app accesses your photo library only when you pick a photo yourself.
  • Export and sharing: labels, PDF or CSV files are only created when you trigger them. You decide where they go.
  • Backups: whether app data is included in a device backup (e.g. iCloud or Google) depends on your device settings; Apple or Google are responsible for this.
  • Deletion: deleting the app removes all data stored in it.

The app is distributed via Apple's App Store and Google Play. When you download it, these providers process data under their own responsibility, which we cannot influence. Their privacy policies apply.

4. Visiting this website

When you visit this website, the web server processes technically necessary information, in particular:

  • IP address of the accessing device
  • date and time of access
  • pages accessed and amount of data transferred
  • browser and operating system used
  • the previously visited page (referrer), if applicable

This processing serves the technical provision, security and stability of the website and the detection of technical errors. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in operating the website securely and reliably. The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer required for these purposes; the specific retention period depends on the hosting provider.

7. Waitlist and wishes by email

The "Notify me" and "Missing something?" forms open your email program with a prepared message. Nothing is stored on the server. Only when you send the email yourself do we receive your name, email address and message.

We use this information only to inform you about the app's launch or to reply to your message, and, if you ticked the box, for news about Cornnector. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time by email. The data is deleted once it is no longer needed for its purpose or if you object.
